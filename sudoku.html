How to Play Sudoku

Sudoku is a logic-based number puzzle played on a 9×9 grid. The goal is to fill every empty cell with numbers from 1 to 9.

Each number can appear only once in every row, column and 3×3 box.

Simple Steps

  • Select an empty cell.
  • Choose a number from 1 to 9.
  • Make sure the number does not already exist in the same row.
  • Check that it does not exist in the same column.
  • Check the 3×3 box as well.
  • Continue until the complete grid is solved.

Sudoku Rules

  • Every row must contain numbers 1 to 9 without repetition.
  • Every column must contain numbers 1 to 9 without repetition.
  • Every 3×3 box must contain numbers 1 to 9 without repetition.
  • Numbers already provided at the beginning cannot be changed.
  • A valid Sudoku has one correct solution.

Sudoku Tips and Strategies

1. Start with Easy Numbers

Look for rows, columns or boxes where only a few cells are empty. These are usually easier to solve first.

2. Check Rows and Columns

Before placing a number, check both its row and column. This helps avoid unnecessary mistakes.

3. Look at 3×3 Boxes

The smaller 3×3 sections are important. Check which numbers are missing and where they can legally fit.

4. Do Not Guess Too Quickly

Sudoku is mainly a logic puzzle. Try to use the information already available on the board before making guesses.

Why Play Sudoku?

Sudoku is a simple way to practice logical thinking and concentration. Solving puzzles regularly can make you more comfortable with identifying patterns and making logical decisions.

  • Practices logical thinking
