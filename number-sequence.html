How to Find Number Patterns

Number sequences can follow different types of patterns. Looking at the difference between consecutive numbers is often a useful first step.

Addition Pattern

2, 4, 6, 8, 10

Each number increases by 2.

Subtraction Pattern

20, 17, 14, 11, 8

Each number decreases by 3.

Multiplication Pattern

2, 4, 8, 16, 32

Each number is multiplied by 2.

Increasing Difference

2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 17

The differences increase by 1 each time: +1, +2, +3, +4, +5.

Number Sequence Tips

1. Check the Difference

Subtract each number from the next one. A repeated difference can reveal a simple pattern.

2. Check Multiplication

If the difference is not constant, check whether the numbers are being multiplied or divided by a common value.

3. Look for Alternating Patterns

Some sequences use two different operations repeatedly. For example, the pattern may alternate between adding and multiplying.

4. Do Not Guess Too Quickly

Look at the entire sequence before choosing an answer. A pattern that fits only the first two numbers may not be the correct pattern.

Number Sequence Strategy

A useful strategy is to solve the easiest pattern first. Start by checking addition and subtraction, then look for multiplication, division or changing differences.

With practice, you may begin to recognize common patterns more quickly. The goal is to understand the relationship between the numbers rather than simply guessing.
